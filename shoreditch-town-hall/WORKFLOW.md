# Shoreditch Town Hall — Scan Workflow Notes

## Planning Authority
**London Borough of Hackney** — planning.hackney.gov.uk

## What Worked

- **Hackney planning register** was the primary and most productive source. Shoreditch Town Hall is Grade II* listed and has been subject to multiple refurbishment and change-of-use applications over the past decade as it transitioned to an arts and events venue. Searching by address (380 Old Street EC1V 9LT) or by the building name returns a cluster of applications, several of which have large architectural drawing sets attached.
- Both PDFs obtained are from planning submissions. The multi-sheet floor plan sets (AH and complete-building) are attached to listed-building-consent or full planning applications, downloadable directly from the Hackney planning portal without registration.
- Hackney's planning portal (built on Idox/Uniform) allows bulk document browsing — clicking into an application's "Documents" tab lists all attachments, making it easy to identify floor plan PDFs by filename convention.

## What Failed / Friction Points

- **File sizes are large** (10 MB and 17 MB) — the Hackney portal occasionally times out on large PDF downloads, requiring retries or direct URL access.
- Some older applications on the Hackney register have documents stored as scanned TIFFs converted to PDF, which are not text-searchable and may be low resolution.
- The venue operator (Shoreditch Town Hall Ltd) does not publish technical floor plans independently — all usable material comes via the planning register.
- No separate stage plan or technical rider document was found for the Assembly Hall, unlike purpose-built arts venues that routinely publish these.

## Best Information Found

`complete-building-floor-plans.pdf` (17.2 MB) is the most comprehensive source — a full multi-level drawing set covering the entire building. `AH-floor-plans.pdf` provides more focused detail on the Assembly Hall block. Between the two files the full spatial layout of the building is well covered. These are the most detailed publicly available architectural drawings for this building.
