# Barbican Centre — Scan Workflow Notes

## Planning Authority
**City of London Corporation** — planning.cityoflondon.gov.uk

## What Worked

- **Venue technical documentation** was the most productive source. The Barbican Centre publishes production rider packs and technical specifications for the Hall, Theatre, and cinemas directly on its website (barbican.org.uk/your-visit/venue-hire). These are freely downloadable without registration and contain genuine architectural-level detail (stage plans, rigging grids, dimension tables).
- **City of London planning register** yielded the planning appendix floor plan set. Searching the register by address (Silk Street EC2Y 8DS) returned prior applications; the appendix PDF was attached to a listed-building-consent or planning application. The CoL register allows document download without an account.
- **2017 venue floor plan** was obtained via the Barbican's public accessibility and event-operations documentation — linked from their Access pages.

## What Failed / Friction Points

- The City of London planning register search UI is date-sensitive — open applications are easy to find but historic applications (pre-2010) require manual reference number lookup via FOI or the Local Studies Library.
- The Barbican is a Grade II listed building owned by the CoL corporation, so some building alterations go through an internal CoL committee process rather than the standard public planning register, making certain drawing sets harder to surface.
- No digital as-built drawings for the residential Barbican Estate towers are publicly available via the planning route — only the arts centre documents were findable.

## Best Information Found

The `barbican_floor_plans_city_of_london_appendix1.pdf` (1.3 MB) is the richest single file — a multi-sheet planning submission with GA plans across multiple levels. Combined with the 2022 stage plan (which gives accurate dimensional data for the concert hall), these two files give the most useful spatial coverage of the building.
