# Imperial College — Sir Alexander Fleming Building Scan Workflow Notes

## Planning Authorities
- **City of London Corporation** — for any works on the eastern edge of the South Kensington campus that fall within CoL boundary (rare)
- **Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea (RBKC)** — planning.rbkc.gov.uk — primary authority for the South Kensington campus, which sits within the Royal Borough

## What Worked

- **Imperial College Estates** publishes campus maps openly on imperial.ac.uk/visit/campuses/south-kensington. The main campus map (south-kensington-campus-map.pdf) was a direct download from that page — no login required.
- **Imperial College Union** publishes its own campus map edition (W23) for students; available from the ICU website or student handbook portal.
- **RBKC planning register** (rbkc.gov.uk/planning) surfaces the issues-and-options consultation document. Searching by "Imperial College" as applicant or by campus postcode (SW7 2AZ) returns relevant planning policy documents and prior applications. RBKC allows document download without registration.
- The **SAFB directions PDF** was sourced from Imperial's own event/visitor information pages, where the college publishes building-specific access guides.

## What Failed / Friction Points

- **No building-level floor plans are publicly available** for the Sir Alexander Fleming Building through any planning register. The SAF building (opened 1998, architect Sheppard Robson) predates widespread digital planning submission, and no substantive refurbishment application with attached floor plans was found on the RBKC register.
- RBKC's planning search is slow and the document viewer requires Adobe-compatible rendering — some older PDFs in the register are scanned and unsearchable.
- Imperial College does not publish room-level floor plans publicly. Internal estate drawings are managed via the college's CAFM (computer-aided facilities management) system, which is not publicly accessible.
- The RBKC local plan consultation document is policy-level only — it contains no floor plans or architectural drawings of the SAF building specifically.

## Best Information Found

The campus map PDFs (both Estates and ICU editions) give accurate building footprint and entrance data. For the SAF building specifically, the directions document (SAFB-directions-imperial-college.pdf) provides the most useful access-level spatial data publicly available. Actual internal floor plans would require an FOI request to Imperial College Estates or RBKC.
